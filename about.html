About DriveEase

Our story, mission, and the team behind every great drive

Our Mission

Making Every Journey Yours

Founded in 2017, DriveEase was born from a simple belief: renting a car should be as easy and enjoyable as driving one. We started with just 12 vehicles in Chennai and have grown to a fleet of 500+ across Tamil Nadu.

Today, we serve over 50,000 customers annually — from solo business travelers to large family vacationers — all united by the DriveEase promise: transparency, quality, and service that goes the extra mile.

What Drives Us

Our Core Values

Transparency

No hidden fees, no surprise charges. Every cost is clearly displayed before you book. We believe honest pricing builds lasting relationships.

Quality

Every vehicle undergoes a 50-point inspection before each rental. We never compromise on safety or cleanliness standards.

Customer First

Our 24/7 support team is always just a call away. Your satisfaction is our top priority — before, during, and after every rental.
